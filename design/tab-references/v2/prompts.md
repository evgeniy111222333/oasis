# ECLIPSE TAB — new visual directions

Built-in ImageGen. Previous designs rejected by user. New concepts remove acquaintance categories and status labels; focus on compact presence roster. Sample names and counts are illustrative.

## Variant 1

Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate a high quality art-directed visual reference for a NEW TAB overlay for ECLIPSE, a Minecraft medieval full-roleplay server. Single landscape 16:9 gameplay screenshot. The UI must be beautiful, restrained, functional and really compact. Minecraft voxel gameplay should occupy at least 70 percent of image visually unobstructed. Actual block-built late medieval stone and timber town seen from first person; subtle nine-slot hotbar bottom. Not realistic RPG or concept painting. No external presentation captions, no multi-panel board, no explanatory diagram.
Design critique to address: oversized leather-bound registers, giant parchment panels, big centered titles, acquainted/unknown columns and repetitive status labels were strongly rejected. Start from a completely new visual direction. UI has ONLY small brand "ECLIPSE", small text "12 у світі", and exactly twelve sample player names. All names illustrative, not claims about game mechanics. No subtitle, no player categories, no unknown IDs, no acquaintances, no masked indicators, no skin faces, no portraits, no profession, no location, no economic info, no power stats, no ping numerals, no repeated connection icons. No instruction footer, no "TAB" label. No search, buttons, tabs, quests or menu navigation. No health bars, inventory, website dashboard or holographic sci-fi.
Names exactly, once each: "Остап", "Еліан", "Ровена", "Марек", "Інгрід", "Альрик", "Торен", "Аделіна", "Ронан", "Северин", "Міра", "Фальк". Clear readable Ukrainian lettering. One small subtle distinction for self "Остап" without a second label. Body font understated legible Cyrillic, narrow and carefully spaced; no enormous fantasy type. Restraint and originality are crucial. Impeccable alignment and proportion. No decorative swords, dragons, giant emblems, thick borders or filigree. No neon. High craft game UI, purposeful graphic design.
Direction A: QUIET CROWN. A tiny floating top-center roster spanning about 56% screen width and exactly 24% screen height, upper edge at 6% screen height. This overlay is extremely low-profile and mostly frameless. Soft dark smoke translucent backdrop fading at edges, no panel boxes around names. Brand left-aligned in a small elegant serif wordmark on header, a 12px eclipse disc at its side. Online count tiny right-aligned. One very fine antique silver header divider. Below, twelve names in four columns and three rows, well spaced vertically, very readable off-white. Ostap is soft ivory with a tiny amber dot; all other names same ink-white. No side badges or stats. Thin vertical dividers ONLY between columns, charcoal and subtle. Type-led design, perhaps a tiny restrained silver ornamental notch ONLY at center of separator. No gold frame. Palette smoke graphite, cool warm-gray white, extremely restrained old amber accent. World backdrop: blue-hour Minecraft town and distant amber windows. Large beautiful visible game scene below. This should feel like a premium lightweight overlay that appears only while holding TAB, rather than a full menu screen. UI scale small, do not enlarge it to fill scene.
